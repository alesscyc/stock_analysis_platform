# Stock Analysis Platform

Terms used for stock research and trading.

## AI chat

**AI chat connection**:
The service URL and API key used to connect the app's AI chat to an AI provider.

## Trading

**Trading mode**:
The active destination for orders: Live mode or Paper mode.

**Live mode**:
Trading through the connected Interactive Brokers account. The broker account determines whether those orders are real or simulated.

**Paper mode**:
Simulated trading in the app's Paper Account, separate from Interactive Brokers.

**Paper Account**:
The app's simulated cash, holdings, and order history. Its orders never reach Interactive Brokers.
